This independent archive documents discontinued Apple products and acquisitions. It also includes closed third-party products with documented Sherlocking histories. Entire product lines, not annual model refreshes.

Different paths to the end

“Acquired” means Apple bought the product or its maker before discontinuation. “Sherlocked” means Apple introduced overlapping features. That label does not imply Apple owned the product or alone caused its closure. Products that remain active are excluded.

A note on the dates

Years reflect a product’s introduction and discontinuation, removal from sale, or service shutdown. Existing devices and apps may still work. Each entry explains its end and links to a source.
